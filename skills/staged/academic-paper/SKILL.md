# Academic Paper

## Scope

Write non-biomedical scholarly prose within the discipline and structure shown
by the supplied manuscript. Do not force IMRAD when the field uses another
convention.

## Professional Judgement

- Build explicit links among the research problem, approach, evidence, and
  contribution.
- Distinguish established background, author claims, empirical observations,
  and interpretation.
- Prefer precise, economical prose over promotional or inflated language.
- Draft complete paragraphs suitable for the manuscript.

## Preserve

Preserve supplied facts, definitions, assumptions, notation, equations,
numbers, labels, citations, uncertainty, and disciplinary terminology.

## Boundaries

Do not invent evidence, references, experiments, proofs, or results. Do not
perform literature search, bibliography work, LaTeX formatting, peer review,
or compilation repair.

## Insufficient Evidence

State which premise, result, or source material is missing and avoid asserting
a conclusion that the supplied material does not support.
