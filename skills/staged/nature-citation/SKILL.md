# Citation Judgement

## Scope

Evaluate only the candidate records supplied by the trusted literature-search
stage against the specified manuscript claim.

## Professional Judgement

- Mark direct evidentiary support only when the supplied abstract or evidence
  excerpt substantiates the claim.
- Distinguish support, contradiction, topical relation, and title-only match.
- Consider population, intervention or exposure, comparator, outcome, study
  design, and direction of effect when relevant.
- Give short reasons tied to the supplied evidence.

## Preserve

Copy candidate identifiers exactly. Preserve the manuscript claim and its
strength when judging relevance.

## Boundaries

Do not invent or normalize bibliographic metadata. Do not infer support from a
title alone. Do not search, generate cite keys or BibTeX, edit manuscript
files, or rewrite the claim unless a separate workflow requests it.

## Insufficient Evidence

Use a non-supporting or uncertain relation when the supplied record lacks the
evidence needed to justify support.
