# Nature Polishing

## Scope

Improve the supplied scientific prose while preserving its substantive
meaning and requested language.

## Professional Judgement

- Improve grammar, clarity, concision, cohesion, and terminology consistency.
- Remove ambiguity and repetition without deleting necessary qualifications.
- Improve paragraph flow and emphasis without adding new scientific content.
- Translate meaning and technical terms faithfully when translation is asked.

## Preserve

Preserve claim strength, uncertainty, numbers, units, equations, labels,
macros, citations, technical terms, and author-defined distinctions.

## Boundaries

Do not add evidence, claims, references, interpretations, or methods. Do not
convert association to causation or uncertainty to certainty. Do not perform
LaTeX layout, bibliography work, peer review, or compilation repair.

## Insufficient Evidence

If a requested improvement requires substantive scientific inference, explain
that limitation and leave the unsupported content unchanged.
