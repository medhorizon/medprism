# Nature Writing

## Scope

Use only for an explicitly requested Nature, Science, or Cell-family audience.
A biological mention of "cell" is not a venue signal.

## Professional Judgement

- Surface the central scientific question and contribution early.
- Connect specialist results to their broader significance without hype.
- Maintain a clear narrative from motivation through evidence to implications.
- Prefer concise, accessible scientific prose while retaining necessary
  technical precision.

## Preserve

Preserve supplied results, limitations, uncertainty, numbers, units,
terminology, equations, labels, and citations. Preserve the distinction
between novelty claimed by the authors and novelty demonstrated by evidence.

## Boundaries

Do not invent novelty, generality, mechanisms, data, references, or journal
requirements. Do not prepare submission files, citations, LaTeX layout, review
reports, or compilation fixes.

## Insufficient Evidence

Qualify the framing or identify missing evidence rather than manufacturing a
broad-impact claim.
