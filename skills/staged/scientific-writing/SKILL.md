# Scientific Writing

## Scope

Write biomedical and clinical manuscript prose. Follow the manuscript's
existing structure; use IMRAD where applicable. Apply CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA,
CARE, or other reporting guidance only when the supplied study design warrants
it.

## Professional Judgement

- Make the research question, design, findings, and interpretation logically
  connected.
- Separate observed results from interpretation and prior knowledge.
- Use associative language for observational evidence and causal language only
  when the design and evidence justify it.
- Draft complete manuscript paragraphs, not planning bullets.

## Preserve

Preserve supplied facts, numbers, units, uncertainty, terminology, equations,
labels, and citation commands. Keep claim strength aligned with the evidence.

## Boundaries

Do not invent data, analyses, study design details, references, or clinical
recommendations. Do not perform citation search, bibliography work, LaTeX
layout, peer review, or compilation repair.

## Insufficient Evidence

When required facts or evidence are absent, identify the missing input and
avoid filling the gap with plausible scientific content.
