MedPrism notes:
- sn-article.tex is a minimal starter that compiles under Tectonic/XeTeX.
- sn-article.official-sample.tex is the official sample with malformed C1
  smart quotes normalized and XeTeX-hostile inline verbatim/listings demos
  neutralized; two lstlisting demo blocks are omitted so it compiles
  reliably in the bundled Tectonic/XeTeX path. The official EPS figure demo
  is kept as fig.eps but replaced with a placeholder inside the sample file,
  because Tectonic/XeTeX cannot include EPS directly.
- Always keep the documentclass [pdflatex,...] option so sn-jnl does not
  load breakurl (incompatible with XeTeX).
